Orrery — Solar Observatory: Privacy Policy

The Perigee Post · Effective October 17, 2026

What the app itself collects

Orrery does not ask for an account, and the app itself collects, stores on a server, or transmits no personal data. There is no sign-in, no server account, and no analytics built into the app.

What stays on your device

Field notes, saved bodies, and display preferences are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Orrery shows a bottom banner ad served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the device advertising ID and related ad-interaction data with Google, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosures. We do not receive or store that data ourselves.

What the app does not access

Orrery does not read your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone.

Children

Orrery is a general-audience educational reference app. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone — the app collects none itself (see above).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
