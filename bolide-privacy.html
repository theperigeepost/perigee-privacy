Bolide: Privacy Policy

The Perigee Post · Effective October 17, 2026

What the game itself collects

Bolide does not ask for an account. Scores, gems, ships, flares, Charter, and Quiet belt stay on this device. Clearing app data removes them. The game itself collects and transmits no personal data and includes no analytics.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

Quiet belt, Charter, and gem bundles are sold through Google Play Billing — Google Play processes the payment; the game never sees a card number. Purchases restore from your Google Play account.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and share the device advertising ID and related ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Quiet belt removes ads. We do not receive or store advertising data ourselves.

What the game does not access

Bolide does not read your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone.

Children

Bolide is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
